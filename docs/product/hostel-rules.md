# Sri Adithya Boys Hostel

## Hostel Residency Agreement – Rules & Regulations

This Hostel Residency Agreement ("Agreement") governs the accommodation provided by Sri Adithya Boys Hostel ("Hostel Management") to the Resident ("Student"). By accepting admission and signing this Agreement, the Student and Parent/Guardian acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with all hostel rules, regulations, policies, and future updates communicated by Hostel Management.

---

# 1. Fee Structure & Payment Policy

## Highlights

* Hostel accommodation is provided for the academic year period of twelve (12) months.
* Hostel fees once paid are strictly non-refundable and non-adjustable under any circumstances.
* If GST or any government-imposed tax becomes applicable to hostel accommodation services, such charges shall be payable by the student in addition to the hostel fee.

## Rules

1. Students must pay the required advance hostel fee at the time of admission as communicated by Hostel Management.
2. Monthly hostel fees must be paid on or before the prescribed due date.
3. Any delay in fee payment may attract a late payment charge of ₹50 per day or such revised amount communicated by Hostel Management.
4. Failure to pay hostel fees within the prescribed timelines may result in penalties, suspension of services, or cancellation of hostel accommodation.
5. All fee payments shall be recorded through the Hostel Management System (HMS) or other approved payment channels.

---

# 2. Accommodation & Hostel Facilities

## Highlights

* Hostel Management is responsible only for providing accommodation and meals (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner).
* Internet/Wi-Fi, Washing Machine, and Hot Water facilities are provided as complimentary amenities and may occasionally face interruptions, maintenance, or downtime.
* Hostel premises may remain closed during semester holidays, festival vacations, and other notified academic breaks. Students must vacate the premises during such periods.

## Rules

1. Complaints regarding internet connectivity, washing machines, or similar facilities may require up to ten (10) days for resolution.
2. Hostel Management reserves the right to change room allocations when operationally necessary.
3. Allocation of the same room throughout the academic year is not guaranteed.
4. Visitors who wish to stay in the hostel may be permitted only with prior approval from Hostel Management and must pay the applicable visitor charges.
5. The current visitor accommodation charge is ₹500 per day unless revised by Hostel Management.

---

# 3. Discipline & Conduct

## Highlights

* Smoking, alcohol consumption, illegal activities, violence, misconduct, and ragging are strictly prohibited.
* Students involved in serious misconduct may be removed from the hostel without refund.

## Rules

1. Students must maintain discipline, respectful behavior, and a healthy hostel environment at all times.
2. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited.
3. Students found participating in ragging, smoking, alcohol consumption, illegal activities, violence, harassment, or other serious misconduct may be immediately removed from the hostel without fee refund.
4. Parents, relatives, friends, visitors, and outsiders are not permitted inside hostel rooms.
5. Visitors may wait only in designated office areas or front lobby spaces.
6. Hostel gate closes at 9:30 PM daily unless special permission is granted by Hostel Management.
7. Students leaving the hostel premises must inform Hostel Management through WhatsApp or any officially designated communication channel.
8. Food is not permitted inside hostel rooms and must be consumed only in designated dining areas.

---

# 4. Safety & Responsibility

## Highlights

* Students are fully responsible for their personal belongings.
* Hostel Management shall not be liable for loss, theft, accidents, injuries, personal disputes, or matters beyond its reasonable control.
* Certain electrical appliances are prohibited for safety reasons.

## Rules

1. Students are solely responsible for safeguarding their mobile phones, laptops, gold, cash, certificates, documents, and other valuables.
2. Hostel Management shall not be responsible for theft, loss, damage, injuries, accidents, personal disputes, personal affairs, or matters occurring outside its reasonable responsibility.
3. Electrical appliances including iron boxes, water heaters, immersion rods, micro-ovens, induction stoves, and other inflammable or high-power devices are strictly prohibited inside hostel rooms unless specifically approved.
4. Students found using prohibited appliances may be fined ₹1,000 per violation or such revised amount notified by Hostel Management.
5. Any damage caused to hostel property, including beds, mattresses, lockers, furniture, fittings, fixtures, equipment, or infrastructure, shall be repaired or compensated by the responsible student(s).

---

# 5. Vacating, Security Deposit & Maintenance Charges

## Highlights

* No refund shall be provided for early vacating under any circumstances.
* Security Deposit requirements are determined by Hostel Management and may vary by hostel or admission policy.
* Maintenance Charges are determined by Hostel Management and may vary by hostel or admission policy.

## Rules

1. Students who vacate the hostel before completion of the academic year shall not be entitled to any hostel fee refund.
2. Hostel charges for students vacating early may be recalculated at the applicable monthly rate communicated by Hostel Management.
3. Students vacating during the academic year must pay the applicable maintenance charges.

# 6. Management Rights

## Highlights

* Hostel Management decisions regarding administration, accommodation, discipline, and operations shall be final and binding.
* Hostel accommodation is a privilege and may be withdrawn for rule violations.

## Rules

1. Hostel Management reserves the full right to discontinue hostel accommodation for any student violating hostel rules and regulations.
2. Hostel Management may take disciplinary action whenever necessary to maintain safety, order, discipline, and operational efficiency.
3. Hostel Management may revise operational policies, accommodation policies, visitor policies, security procedures, and facility usage guidelines whenever required.
4. Continued stay in the hostel constitutes acceptance of such revised policies and regulations.
