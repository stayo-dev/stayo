# Overview

StayO is a modern Property Management System (PMS) built specifically for student hostels and co-living spaces in India.

Unlike traditional PMS platforms that target hotels or apartments, StayO focuses on solving the day-to-day operational problems faced by hostel owners. Many hostel owners still rely on WhatsApp, Excel sheets, notebooks, and manual registers to manage admissions, rent collection, room allocation, food management, complaints, and tenant communication.

StayO replaces these disconnected workflows with a single platform that enables hostel owners to manage their entire business from one dashboard while providing tenants with a dedicated portal for daily interactions.

The first version of StayO is intentionally focused on hostel operations rather than marketplace discovery. Owners already have their own tenants and continue their existing admission process. StayO begins managing operations after the owner decides to onboard a tenant.

---

# Vision

To become the operating system for student accommodation by digitizing every operational workflow inside a hostel.

Instead of building multiple standalone tools for admissions, payments, complaints, food, and communication, StayO combines these workflows into one integrated platform.

The long-term vision is that every hostel owner should be able to manage their business without maintaining physical registers or spreadsheets.

---

# Problem Statement

Current hostel operations are highly manual.

Typical owner workflow today:

• Tenant contacts owner.
• Owner shares hostel details over WhatsApp.
• Admission details are collected manually.
• Payments are recorded in notebooks or Excel.
• Complaints arrive through phone calls.
• Food schedules are shared manually.
• Due reminders depend on the owner's memory.
• Reports require manual calculations.

These disconnected processes create several problems:

- Lost payment records
- Missed rent reminders
- Room allocation confusion
- Difficult reporting
- No centralized tenant database
- Poor communication between owner and tenants

StayO exists to eliminate these operational inefficiencies.

# Target Users

## Hostel Owner

Primary decision maker.

Responsibilities:

- Manage hostels
- Add rooms
- Invite tenants
- Track payments
- Handle complaints
- Manage food schedules
- View reports
- Configure hostel settings

The owner is the primary user in Version 1.

---

## Tenant

Invited by the owner.

Responsibilities:

- View room details
- View payment history
- Raise complaints
- Vote on food polls
- View food schedule
- Receive announcements

Tenants cannot create hostels or manage operational settings.

---

## Admin (Internal)

Not included in Version 1.

Internal operations only.

Responsible for:

- Approving owner registrations
- Managing subscriptions
- Supporting customers

No production UI exists yet.

# Product Principles

1. Mobile-first experience.

2. Reduce owner workload.

3. Require minimum tenant training.

4. Every action should require as few clicks as possible.

5. Reuse the existing backend wherever possible.

6. Build a completely new frontend while preserving existing business logic.
