---

# Pricing Philosophy

StayO is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform built specifically for hostel owners.

The pricing model should be:

- Simple to understand
- Easy to calculate
- Scalable with hostel growth
- Affordable for small hostels
- Sustainable for StayO

Hostel owners should never need a calculator to estimate their monthly or yearly cost.

---

# Revenue Model

StayO follows a subscription-based pricing model.

Revenue is generated from:

- Annual platform subscription
- Per-active-tenant pricing

This ensures pricing scales naturally as the hostel grows.

---

# Version 1 Pricing

## Platform Fee

Every hostel pays a fixed annual platform fee.

Example

₹500 / year

This covers:

- Platform access
- Software updates
- Technical support
- Security updates
- Dashboard access

---

## Per Active Tenant

Owners pay only for active tenants.

Example

₹40 per active tenant

Examples

50 tenants

₹500 + (50 × ₹40)

100 tenants

₹500 + (100 × ₹40)

200 tenants

₹500 + (200 × ₹40)

The total billing amount increases only when the hostel grows.

# Customer Onboarding

1. Owner visits StayO website.

2. Owner submits registration.

3. StayO team reviews application.

4. Pricing is discussed manually.

5. Agreement is finalized.

6. Owner receives activation link.

7. Owner completes onboarding.

8. Subscription begins.

---

# Trial Policy

Version 1 does not include self-service free trials.

Demo access may be provided manually by the StayO team.

Future versions may introduce:

- 7-day trial
- 14-day trial
- Limited feature trial

---

# Payment Collection

Version 1 supports manual payment collection.

Possible methods:

- UPI
- Bank Transfer
- Cash (if required)

After payment confirmation, the subscription is activated by the StayO team.

Future versions may support online payment gateways.

---

# Subscription Renewal

Owners receive reminders before subscription expiry.

Suggested reminder schedule:

30 days before expiry

15 days before expiry

7 days before expiry

1 day before expiry

Renewal restores uninterrupted platform access.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Owners may cancel their subscription.

Data handling after cancellation:

- Dashboard access disabled
- Data retained for a defined retention period
- Reactivation possible without data loss (subject to policy)

Permanent deletion follows legal and operational requirements.

---

# Refund Policy

Version 1 refunds are handled manually.

Future versions may automate refund workflows based on company policy.

---

# Discounts

Version 1 discounts are manually approved.

Examples

Early customers

Educational institutions

Pilot partnerships

Bulk hostel operators

No automatic discount engine exists in Version 1.

# Billing Principles

Pricing should always be:

Transparent

Predictable

Scalable

Fair

No hidden charges should exist.

Customers should always know how their bill is calculated.

---

# Pricing Review

Pricing should be reviewed:

- Annually
- After major feature releases
- Based on customer feedback
- Based on operational costs

Changes should not affect existing customers without prior communication.

---

# Success Metrics

The pricing model should achieve:

- Easy customer understanding
- High renewal rate
- Sustainable revenue
- Low billing disputes
- Long-term customer retention

---

# Summary

StayO follows a simple subscription model consisting of a fixed annual platform fee combined with per-active-tenant pricing.

This approach keeps pricing affordable for small hostels while allowing revenue to scale as customers grow.
