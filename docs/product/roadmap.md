Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product roadmap for StayO.

It outlines how the platform will evolve from Version 1 into a comprehensive hostel operating system.

The roadmap serves as a strategic planning document rather than a development schedule.

Features may move between releases based on customer feedback, technical priorities, and business needs.

---

# Product Vision

StayO aims to become the operating system for student hostels.

The platform will help hostel owners manage every aspect of their business through a single, modern, cloud-based application.

Every release should move the product closer to this vision.

# Guiding Principles

Every roadmap decision should:

Improve owner productivity.

Reduce manual work.

Increase transparency.

Enhance tenant experience.

Support long-term scalability.

Maintain simplicity.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following are intentionally excluded from the MVP:

- Online hostel discovery
- Student marketplace
- Online payments
- Visitor management
- Inventory management
- AI recommendations
- Multi-hostel ownership
- Staff accounts
- Referral system
- Loyalty programs

These features will be evaluated in future releases.

---

# Roadmap Review

The roadmap should be reviewed:

- After every major release
- After significant customer feedback
- Before planning a new version

Changes should be documented with version history.

---

# Summary

StayO will evolve from a hostel management platform into a complete hostel operating system.

Version 1 focuses on operational excellence.

Future versions will introduce automation, intelligence, and marketplace capabilities while preserving the core principles of simplicity, reliability, and operational efficiency.
